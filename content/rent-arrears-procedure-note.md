# Working a rent-arrears case

Use this note when a rent account falls into arrears and you've been assigned the case. A
case is worked correctly when you've contacted the tenant, recorded the outcome on the
ledger, agreed a way forward or escalated it, and the next action is diarised. Follow the
steps in order.

## Before you start

Have these to hand before you begin:

- the rent account ledger for the tenancy
- the tenancy record, including the tenancy type and start date
- any prior contact notes on the case

## Steps

1. Open the ledger and confirm the arrears figure and how far back it goes.
2. Check the tenancy record for anything that changes how you act — a live repayment
   arrangement, a Housing Benefit or Universal Credit claim, or a support flag.
3. Contact the tenant by phone or in writing, set out the balance owed and the date it
   fell due, and ask them to clear it or agree a repayment plan.
4. If the tenant responds, agree a realistic repayment amount, record it on the ledger and
   diarise the first payment date. If the tenant doesn't respond within 10 working days,
   send a first warning letter and diarise a follow-up for 14 days later.
5. If the arrears reach 8 weeks' rent and there's no agreement, refer the case to your team
   leader to consider serving a Notice of Seeking Possession — the formal notice that
   warns the tenant the council may ask the court to end the tenancy.

## Finish the case

Confirm the ledger shows the contact you made, the outcome and the next action, and that
the follow-up date is in your diary. If you can't complete a step — for example the
tenancy record is missing or the ledger doesn't reconcile — note what's blocking it on the
case and pass it to your team leader rather than closing the case.
